# 💐 FNP (Ferns and Petals) Sales Dashboard – Excel Project
![FNP Logo](https://github.com/132006-bhumi/fnp-sales-analysis/blob/main/FNP%20logo.png)
# 🔍 Overview
This project involves a detailed sales analysis of Ferns and Petals (FNP), a gifting service catering to special occasions such as birthdays, anniversaries, and festivals like Diwali and Raksha Bandhan. Using Excel, an interactive dashboard was created to visualize key sales metrics including revenue by category, customer behavior, and product performance across different times and locations. The analysis aims to support strategic decision-making for marketing, logistics, and product offerings.
# 🎯 Objectives
• To calculate total revenue, total orders, and average customer spending.

• To assess monthly sales trends and revenue variations across key festivals and occasions.

• To evaluate delivery performance through average order-to-delivery time.

• To identify the top 5 revenue-generating products and their popularity.

• To determine customer behavior patterns by hour, city, and occasion.

• To provide actionable insights for sales strategy improvement.
# ❓ Business Questions Addressed
1. What is the total revenue generated?

2. What is the average time taken between order and delivery?

3. How do monthly sales vary over the year?

4. Which products generated the most revenue?

5. What is the average customer spending?

6. How do the top 5 products perform in terms of sales?

7. Which cities placed the highest number of orders?

8. Is there a relationship between order quantity and delivery time?

9. How does revenue differ across occasions?

10. Which products are most popular for each occasion?
# 📌 Findings and Conclusion
•Total Orders placed: 1,000

• Total Revenue: ₹35,20,984

• Average Customer Spend: ₹3,520.98

• Average Order-Delivery Time: 5.53 days

• February and August saw the highest revenue spikes, likely due to Valentine’s Day and Raksha Bandhan.

• Soft Toys and Sweets generated high revenue, but Colors stood out as a surprise category leader.

• The top 5 products included Magnum Set, Quia Gift, and Deserunt Box, each generating ₹1,00,000+ revenue.

• Imphal and Kavali contributed the highest revenue among cities.

• Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan were the best-performing occasions.

• Sales peak around afternoon and evening hours, based on order-time analysis.
# Conclusion:
The dashboard reveals that customer spending patterns are highly occasion-driven, with strong peaks during Valentine’s Day, Raksha Bandhan, and August in general. Cities like Imphal and Kavali present untapped market potential. The FNP team can leverage these insights for seasonal promotions and inventory planning.
# 📈 Strategic Insights Summary
This Excel dashboard provides a clear, data-driven analysis of gifting trends across time, geography, and product categories for Ferns and Petals. By uncovering customer spending patterns, seasonal demand spikes, and city-wise sales performance, it equips business leaders with actionable insights to refine marketing strategies, optimize inventory planning, and enhance customer engagement during key occasions.
